- **`Block::Hanging { marker: n, .. }` marks a paragraph that starts with a marker of its own, `n` bytes long.** Its wrapped lines start under the text after the marker, as a Markdown list item's do after `- [ ] ` or `12. `, even when a word too wide for the rest of the first line starts on the next; the first line keeps the margin. It edits as a `Block::Paragraph`. A marker that ends past the first line, or a right-to-left first line, keeps every line at the margin. Apps that `match` on `Block` need the new arm.
