- **Off the Mac, Ctrl+arrows move by word and Home and End by line in text fields.** Linux and
  Windows report Ctrl as the primary key, so Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right had taken the Mac's
  Cmd+arrow meaning and jumped to the line's edge, and Home and End went to the text's ends.
  Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End still go to the text's ends; macOS is unchanged.
