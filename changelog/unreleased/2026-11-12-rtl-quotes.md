- **Right-to-left quotes draw their bar on the right.** A quoted paragraph that reads right to left, a `Block::Quote` or a quoted `Block::Bullet` or `Block::Hanging`, draws its quote bar at the right margin, where its text starts, as far in as a left-to-right quote's bar stands from the left. A quote whose paragraphs read both ways draws one bar for each run of paragraphs that read the same way, each on its own side. A quoted paragraph with no strong character, such as an empty or digits-only line, draws its bar on the side of the paragraph before it in the quote, else the one after it, so a new empty line in a right-to-left quote keeps the bar whole; a quote with no letters at all draws it on the left.
