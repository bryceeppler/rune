- **A text of several paragraphs lays out one paragraph at a time, so typing into a long note shapes only the paragraph it changes.** Each hard paragraph (text between `\n`s) is its own cached Parley layout, stacked under the text's `TextLayout`, and an edit's stack starts from the one before. In a 40 KB, 3,091-line notes editor this took a typed character's frame from 31 ms to under 3 ms. Carets, hits, line moves, selection, decorations, pills, glyphs and accessibility lines match one layout of the same text. One difference can show: with line heights off the pixel grid (a heading, or 1.5x), a wrapped paragraph's lines below its first round inside the paragraph and can sit a device pixel from where one layout put them. Text with right-to-left characters, unsorted spans, or centred lines without a wrap width lays out whole as before. `TextEdit::key` takes `Option<&TextLayout>` for its layout. A field's rich spans rebuild once per view instead of once per builder call, and span backgrounds, underlines and pill chips paint only the paragraphs the clip shows.
