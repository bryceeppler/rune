- **An input method composition finishes in the field it began in.** Moving focus off a field mid-composition (a click, Tab, a focus request, the field going away, or the window deactivating) now ends the platform's composition on macOS and Windows too, and the commit or cancel the input method sends lands in that field and runs its `on_input_change`, never in the field that took focus. A field whose model was replaced or removed hears nothing of it. `App::on_ime` editors follow the same rule, so a commit that follows the preedit clearing reaches the app instead of the field.
