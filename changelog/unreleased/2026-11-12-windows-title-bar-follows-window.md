- **Windows: a window's title bar takes the window's own background.** Rune paints the native title bar (DWM's caption colour) in the colour the window shows behind its content and turns its dark mode on where white text reads better on that colour, so a light window on a dark system gets a light title bar and a dark one a title bar in its own shade. It follows each change of that colour, a theme switch or the system's appearance included, and keeps the window's mode through every `WM_SETTINGCHANGE`, after which winit sets the system's mode again. Before, the title bar always followed the system's light or dark setting.
