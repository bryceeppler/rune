- **A `Block::Hanging` paragraph can sit inside a quote.** `Block::Hanging { marker, level, quote }` with `quote` set draws the quote's bar, one bar with the `Block::Quote` lines around it, and starts its levels from the quote's indent, so a numbered or checklist item written inside a Markdown quote indents by level within the quote and wraps under its text. `Block::quoted` says whether a paragraph draws the bar. Code that builds a `Block::Hanging` passes `quote`.
