- **Right-to-left list items draw their markers on the right.** A `Block::Bullet` or `Block::Numbered` paragraph that reads right to left indents from the right and draws its dot or number (`.1`) in the right gutter, and a `Block::Hanging` one wraps under its text from the right. A pill's label no longer decides its paragraph's direction, so `- [x] שלום` reads right to left; a pill that starts a right-to-left paragraph draws its chip and icon on the right, where a press lands. A click left of a right-to-left line puts the caret at the line's end instead of the next line's start.
