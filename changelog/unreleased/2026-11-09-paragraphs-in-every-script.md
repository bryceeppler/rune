- A text of several paragraphs lays out each paragraph on its own in every script and direction, so typing in a long note written in Japanese, or holding Arabic or Hebrew, reshapes only the edited paragraph (a 50 KB note: about 12 and 23 ms a keystroke to 0.25 ms). A `\n` now ends a paragraph for the bidirectional algorithm, and every paragraph takes the whole text's direction: a neutral at a paragraph's edge (a list dash, a closing `!`) no longer takes the direction of the paragraphs around it, and spaces, digits and punctuation starting a paragraph take its own script, so they can kern with the text after them.
