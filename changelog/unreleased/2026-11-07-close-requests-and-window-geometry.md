- **Apps decide when they close, and reopen where they were.** `App::on_close_request` asks before a window closes or the app quits and takes its answer now or later, from any thread, once a save ends: macOS Quit, ⌘Q, the Dock and logout wait through AppKit's reply-later termination, and the new `Tx::request_quit` asks every window on every platform. A Windows session end and `SIGTERM` now run `on_close` before the process ends instead of killing it. `App::on_geometry`, `WindowHandle::geometry` and `App::restore_geometry` save and restore a window's frame, state and display, moved onto a display present at launch. `Shell` gains `RequestQuit`, so an exhaustive `match` on it needs an arm.
