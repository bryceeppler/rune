- **Left and Right move a text field's caret the way the text draws.** Left always moves toward the screen's left, in a right-to-left paragraph, through an English word inside Hebrew or Arabic, and through a Hebrew phrase inside English. Before, Left stepped back in the text, so it moved the caret right in right-to-left text. Shift+Left and Shift+Right select the same way, and Alt (Ctrl off macOS) with an arrow stops at the left or right edge of each word as drawn. Where the text changes direction, one character position draws in two places, and the caret stops at both, drawn where it stopped. Off a line's edge the caret goes on in reading order: in a right-to-left paragraph Left goes to the next line's right edge. Left on a selection collapses it to its end drawn furthest left, and Right to the one drawn furthest right; a selection over lines collapses to its start or end as its paragraph reads. The caret keeps its side through a click, Up and Down, and an app setting the same selection again, and Up and Down start from where it draws. On macOS, Cmd+Left and Cmd+Right go to a line's left and right ends as it draws, and at a soft wrap Cmd+Right stops at the line's own end rather than the next line's start. Ctrl+A, B, E and F on macOS still move through the text in its order, and Home and End still go to a line's start and end in reading order.
