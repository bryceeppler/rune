- **Left and Right move a text field's caret the way the text draws.** Left always moves toward the screen's left, in a right-to-left paragraph, through an English word inside Hebrew or Arabic, and through a Hebrew phrase inside English. Before, Left stepped back in the text, so it moved the caret right in right-to-left text. Shift+Left and Shift+Right select the same way, and Alt (Ctrl off macOS) with an arrow stops at the left or right edge of each word as drawn. Where the text changes direction, one character position draws in two places, and the caret stops at both, drawn where it stopped. Off a line's edge the caret goes on in reading order: in a right-to-left paragraph Left goes to the next line's right edge. Left on a selection collapses it to its start in a left-to-right paragraph and to its end in a right-to-left one, and Right the other way. Ctrl+B and Ctrl+F on macOS still move back and forward in the text. Home, End, Up and Down are unchanged.
